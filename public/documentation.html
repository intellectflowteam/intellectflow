1. Executive Summary & Product Vision

IntellectFlow solves the core review growth bottleneck for Indian local shopkeepers (tea stalls, salons, clinics, restaurants, showrooms). It replaces enterprise tools charging ₹8,000–₹1,08,000/month with an automated ₹299/month system.

The Core Triad Mechanism:

  1. Zero Friction Scanning: Counter-ready QR standees scanned by any smartphone camera.
  2. Smart 5★ Routing Engine: 5★ ratings invoke Gemini AI review writing & redirect to Google Maps. 1–3★ ratings block Google completely and catch feedback in a private owner inbox.
  3. 25 Automation Tools: AI review replies, GMB posts, FAQs, competitor 2km auto-fetch, and local SEO scores.

3. Deep-Dive Database Schemas (Supabase RLS)

All database tables reside under `public` schema in PostgreSQL with strict Row Level Security:

Core Tables Summary:

  • `public.profiles`: `id`, `email`, `plan` (starter, growth, pro), `plan_price`, `is_admin`. Protected by owner RLS `(auth.uid() = id)`.
  • `public.businesses`: `id`, `user_id`, `name`, `slug` (unique), `gmb_link`, `rating`, `total_reviews`. Read policy `Public read businesses by slug` allows anon QR scanning.
  • `public.reviews`: `id`, `business_id`, `rating`, `review_text`, `ai_generated`, `status` (pending, private, public, replied), `sentiment` (positive, neutral, negative).
  • `public.gmb_posts` & `public.competitors`: GMB updates & pinned competitors with AI SWOT JSON.
